## Core results

Defines **MiP-Overthinking**: on ill-posed questions with a missing essential premise, reasoning
models' response length *explodes* (2–4× beyond general overthinking) while abstain rates stay
low. Four curated datasets (MiP-Formula, MiP-SVAMP, MiP-GSM8K, MiP-MATH; rule-based generation,
body-question swapping, essential-premise removal). Models: QwQ-32B, DeepSeek-R1 + distills, S1.1,
GPT-o1/o1-mini/o3-mini vs. non-reasoning Qwen2.5-32B, Gemma-2-27B, Phi-3, GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5.

- R1/QwQ go from ~1,000–1,800 tokens on well-defined GSM8K questions to >3,000 on MiP variants;
  **non-reasoning models keep roughly constant length (~200 tokens) and abstain more reliably**.
- Token-pattern analysis: explosive growth in "alternatively/wait/check" tokens and step counts
  under MiP; step-level cosine similarity rises (0.45→0.50 with collapsed variance) — the extra
  length is *repetition and self-doubt loops*, not analysis.
- **The killer finding (§4.1):** models *suspect* the missing premise early (high In-Process
  Suspicion Rate, low First Suspicion Index) but do not commit — they keep re-visiting the
  question instead of abstaining. Detection exists; the *act* is missing.
- GPT-o1 abstains notably better than open reasoning models — frontier training partially fixes
  this.
- Overthinking is **contagious through distillation**: fine-tuning Qwen2.5-7B on just 50 R1 MiP
  responses transplants the length-explosion and lowered abstain rate.

## Relevance to A/B/C

- **A — direct (the S3 mechanism).** Underspecified input doesn't shorten work, it inflates it —
  and the model already *knows* mid-trace. For our loop: a planner given a slice.md with unsettled
  premises will burn tokens circling rather than raising `questions`, unless raising them early is
  made an explicit, legitimate, cheap move in its contract.
- **B/C — none directly.**

## Supported interventions

1. **License the act, don't add detection** (catalogue A1's evidence core): give the planner an
   explicit early-exit/clarify slot (the `questions` hand-back exists — the register should
   declare that using it *early* is correct behavior, not failure).
2. **Slice.md completeness gate before planning:** ill-posedness is the trigger, so the cheap fix
   is upstream — triage/refinement pins premises before the plan loop spends tokens (this is what
   /dev:plan-slice's refinement session is for; the evidence says it's load-bearing).
3. Watch for the self-doubt token signature (wait/alternatively loops, high step-similarity) as a
   transcript-level overthinking indicator (instrumentation flavor, I2/I3).

## Applicability caveats

Math QA only; open reasoning models are 32B-class, and the best-behaved model tested (GPT-o1)
suggests frontier models like Opus 5 may already abstain better; response-length metrics don't
map 1:1 onto an agentic loop where "length" is tool-call turns. The distillation result is
training-time.

## Briefing check

Confirmed — ill-posed/premise-missing tasks inflate reasoning drastically instead of triggering
clarification. The actionable nuance the briefing's phrasing misses: the models *do notice* the
missing premise early and fail only to act on it — so the intervention target is the output
contract (make abstain/clarify a first-class move), not better gap detection.
