## Core results

ProCo (mask a "key condition" in the question, build a substitute verification question, check equivalence, iterate verify→correct) is compared against Self-Correct ("review previous answer and find mistakes") on GPT-3.5-Turbo-1106, GPT-4-0125-Preview, and Mixtral-8x7B.

- **Open-domain QA / commonsense** (Table main-gpt3.5, Sec 5.2): ProCo beats Self-Correct by +7.9 (NQ EM), +7.4 (TriviaQA), +7.8 (WebQ), +4.0 (HotpotQA), +9.6 (CSQA accuracy), GPT-3.5.
- **Arithmetic reasoning** (Table 2): GPT-3.5: GSM8K 87.1 vs 75.1, AQuA 65.2 vs 48.7, MATH 41.5 vs 27.6 — avg +14.1 vs Self-Correct.
- **GPT-4 backbone** (Table 3): GSM8K 97.6 vs 91.5, CSQA 86.7 vs 79.5, HotpotQA EM 61.0 vs 49.0 — gains persist but shrink with a stronger model.
- **Error-introduction rate** (Sec 5.3, GSM8K, 3 rounds, GPT-3.5): Self-Correct breaks a *correct* answer 9.1% of the time and fixes an incorrect one 7.6% of the time; ProCo breaks correct answers only 2.5% of the time and fixes incorrect ones 8.2% of the time. This is the paper's clearest kind-of-difference result, not just magnitude.
- **Convergence/iteration behavior** (Fig. 4a, App. 7.5): more Self-Correct rounds barely help and can hurt (TriviaQA −1.0 EM); ProCo improves monotonically, average iterations <3, and per-iteration token/time cost *decreases* as the loop converges (WebQ: 3446 vs 1368 total tokens for Self-Correct vs ProCo).
- **Efficiency vs. retrieval** (Table 4): ProCo+1 doc beats GenRead/RAG+5 docs by 12.8%/9.2% EM using ~half the tokens.

## Relevance to A/B/C

- **A (upfront complexity routing)**: none directly — no task-classification experiment. Indirect resonance only: ProCo's effort is decided *by outcome of a cheap check*, not by upfront estimation, and this adaptive stopping is what shrinks cost over rounds (App. 7.5) — a different lever than the routing the operator's experiment tried.
- **B (comment churn / claim-weakening)**: relevant. Self-Correct's open-ended critique is the paper's explicit foil for "vague, unfounded" flagging; ProCo's proposition-based check (not exact-match) is built specifically to avoid misclassifying correct-but-differently-worded answers as wrong (the "B. Traven" example, App. 7.4) — a direct analogue to penalizing a comment for wording drift rather than substance.
- **C (unbounded work amplification)**: relevant. Self-Correct shows no guaranteed convergence and can regress with more rounds; ProCo's verify-then-correct converges in <3 iterations with falling marginal cost. Evidence that untargeted critique loops don't self-limit, targeted-condition loops do.

## Supported interventions

1. Reviewers verify a **named, specific claim** via an equivalence/proposition check ("is X still true, yes/no"), not open critique — backed by the 8-dataset ProCo>Self-Correct gap and the 9.1%→2.5% error-introduction drop.
2. Use **semantic equivalence, not literal match**, when checking a claim — backed by App. 7.4's exact-match-misclassifies-correct-answers case; directly cautions against penalizing wording drift (the "will"→"may" pattern in B).
3. **Exit the loop once a condition verifies true**, rather than continuing to iterate — backed by ProCo's early-exit stopping rule and falling per-round token cost.
4. **Carry forward a "known-rejected" set** as feedback to stop repeat findings — backed by ProCo's P_t mechanism ("the answer is likely not in {P}"), addressing churn (C).

## Applicability caveats

Tasks are short (avg. 52.3 words, paper's own Limitations), single ground-truth-answer problems (number/entity/concept) — nothing like multi-file code review or subjective quality judgments. Models are GPT-3.5/4-era and Mixtral-8x7B (2024), not Opus 5. Max 3 iterations tested. The "key condition" mechanism requires a single crisply maskable, checkable token — code correctness/quality criteria are usually compound and not reducible this way, so transfer to a coder/reviewer loop is extrapolation, not tested.

## Briefing check

Confirm, with a sharpening. The paper does show structured, specific-condition verification behaves differently from open-ended critique — not just in magnitude (+4 to +16.5 points) but in kind (lower error-introduction rate, guaranteed-ish convergence vs. possible regression). But the mechanism is narrower than "be specific": it's mask-a-condition-into-a-fill-in-the-blank-question plus an equivalence check against a reconstructable answer — not merely narrowing critique scope.
