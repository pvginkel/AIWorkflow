# UX Review Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing a recommendation.

## Core Fit

- Is the user goal explicit?
- Is the recommended direction clearly stated?
- Does the proposal solve the stated problem rather than just restyle the UI?

## Flow and Clarity

- Is the entry point clear?
- Is the next action obvious at each step?
- Is the information ordered by importance?
- Is unnecessary complexity removed?

## Feedback and Safety

- Does the UI show loading, success, and error feedback?
- Are destructive actions protected?
- Can the user recover from mistakes?
- Are validation rules clear and timely?

## Accessibility

- Can the flow be completed with a keyboard?
- Are labels, instructions, and errors understandable to assistive technology?
- Is focus handling described for dialogs, validation, and step changes?
- Are touch targets and contrast considerations addressed when relevant?

## Responsive Behavior

- Is the mobile behavior described?
- Is content priority preserved on smaller screens?
- Are touch interactions or condensed layouts covered where needed?

## Implementation Readiness

- Can a frontend developer identify the required components?
- Are state transitions and edge cases specified?
- Are assumptions clearly marked?
- Are open questions separated from firm recommendations?
